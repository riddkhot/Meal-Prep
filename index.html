Basic meal prep tips

🗓️ Pick a prep day

Set aside 1–2 hours, usually Sunday. Batch-cook grains and proteins first, since they take longest.

📦 Use good containers

Glass containers with sectioned lids keep sauces separate and reheat evenly.

❄️ Mind the fridge clock

Most cooked meals last 3–4 days. Freeze portions for later in the week.

🥗 Keep crunch separate

Store dressings, granola and crispy toppings on the side until you eat.

🔁 Rotate weekly

Swap 1–2 recipes each week so you don't burn out on the same meals.

🌡️ Cool before sealing

Let food cool for about 30 minutes before you put lids on. This stops sogginess and condensation.
